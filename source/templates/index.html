1. Researching the brief

“Data is the new oil. Like oil, data is valuable, but if unrefined it cannot really be used.” - Clive Humby

I think this quote extends further than the literal interpretation of data being valuable. In the age of advertising, data is the modern day oil. When services are free, you are the product. Tracking information can make or break a corporation.

The refining comes down to data analysis. Unrefined data is simply a list of strings and integers. The value is born when we learn from those lists.

1) Advertising

To respond directly to the brief I aim to find a dataset that is seemingly superfluous at first glance, but can gain meaning if tweaked correctly. This would be evident in datasets such as social media retention rates---when did someone click off a video? What thumbnails received more traffic? Where does that traffic come from? These features are simple to track, but go a long way if analysed correctly.
